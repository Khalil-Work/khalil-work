# Custom cover prompts

Built-in image-generation tool was used for the original artwork and the customised editorial text. No reference artwork was copied. The following is the final prompt set and artwork specification.

## Shared original art direction

Original polished editorial 3D artwork for a mechanical-engineering research portfolio. Wide landscape composition, dark ink-navy background, sophisticated violet accents, convincing materials and light, strong silhouette, breathing room. Symbolic brand artwork, not a scientific result. No text, labels, plot, interface, card outline, border or watermark. Smooth forms; avoid a flat generic icon, cartoon or excessive neon.

| Cover | Subject |
| --- | --- |
| Vortex Notebook | Intricate fluid vortex sculpted from translucent indigo and smoked-glass ribbons in a helical toroidal eddy; secondary filaments and silver streamlines; asymmetric sweep and depth. |
| Shockline | Titanium aerodynamic wing form at an oblique angle with translucent smoky-violet shock-wave sheets and champagne-gold edges; controlled highlights and momentum. |
| Droplet Journey | Optically detailed teal liquid droplet above a shallow meniscus, violet caustics, vapor wisps and a few smaller droplets; macro product-photography finish. |
| Triangle Mesh Audit | Flowing saddle-like shell as a dense triangulated titanium lattice with fine silver struts, restrained violet edges and a few champagne nodes; curved geometry and negative space. |

## Final customisation prompt

Edit the existing project artwork into a customised research-project cover for KHALIL. Preserve the subject, materials, colours, quality and dark background. Add the exact project wordmark, short subject line and small KHALIL signature below. Use sophisticated clean sans-serif typography, white main title and pale-lavender secondary text. Create quiet negative space at the left and place the sculpture mainly on the right. Keep generous safe margins, allow a two-line title, and make the wordmark readable at phone size. No other words, charts, axes, boxes, borders, badges, numbers or slogans. Keep a wide landscape composition and give the artwork the majority of visual attention. This is portfolio concept art, not a result graphic.

| Shockline | One-dimensional Euler waves | KHALIL |
